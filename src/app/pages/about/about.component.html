flag Our Mission

Giving clinics back their time

Healthcare providers spend an average of 2–3 hours per day on administrative tasks — scheduling, reminders, records, billing coordination. That's time stolen from patients.

CareCore exists to eliminate that friction. We build tools that handle the operational side of running a clinic so that doctors, nurses, and clinic staff can focus entirely on care.

We're a remote-first team serving clinics across Southeast Asia and beyond, hosted on secure AWS infrastructure.
