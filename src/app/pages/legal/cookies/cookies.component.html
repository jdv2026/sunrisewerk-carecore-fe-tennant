info This Cookie Policy explains how CareCore uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies when you use our platform.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. CareCore also uses browser storage mechanisms (localStorage and sessionStorage) which function similarly to cookies to keep your session active and remember your preferences.

2. How We Use Cookies

CareCore uses only essential cookies and browser storage. We do not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking cookies.

Authentication & Session

  • Store your login session tokens issued by AWS Cognito
  • Keep you signed in between page visits
  • Invalidate your session securely when you sign out

Preferences

  • Remember your "Remember me" login preference
  • Store user interface settings (e.g. selected language or timezone)

3. Types of Storage We Use

Depending on your login preference:

  • localStorage: Used when you select "Remember me" — your session persists across browser restarts until you sign out
  • sessionStorage: Used when you do not select "Remember me" — your session is cleared when you close the browser tab

No persistent tracking cookies are set on your device.

4. Third-Party Cookies

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google may set its own cookies as part of the OAuth authentication flow. These are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. CareCore does not control or have access to these cookies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies will prevent CareCore from functioning correctly — you will not be able to remain signed in.

To clear your CareCore session manually, use the Sign Out option within the application.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We will notify registered users by email if changes are material.
